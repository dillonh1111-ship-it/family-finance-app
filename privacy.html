Family Finance Privacy Policy

Family Finance is a private tool used only by the Hunt family to organize household receipts and budgets. It is not offered to the public.

With the account owner's permission, it reads receipt files in the family's own Google Drive and records spending in the family's own private Google Sheet. Data is used only for the family's budgeting.

No data is sold, shared, or used for advertising. Access can be revoked at any time from the Google Account's security settings.
